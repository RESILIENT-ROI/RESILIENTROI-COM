- **Claim:** BPTAPP is described as "currently travelling on our own in-house BPT instance."
- **Why it matters:** It's unclear to a buyer whether this means the tool is proven only internally (not yet client-deployed) or already running at client sites. If it's the former, saying so plainly protects credibility; if read as implying client deployment when there isn't one yet, that's an overclaim risk for a buyer doing diligence. (Note: recent commit history shows this wording may already be mid-revision — "Rephrase BPTAPP to future tense — it's not operational yet" — so this may be a known work-in-progress.)

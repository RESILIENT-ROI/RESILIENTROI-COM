
# /buyer-fit Report

Fetched the now-live resilientroi.com (deploy confirmed successful — hero tagline, Knowledge/Hands-On Presence cards, updated nav, team bios and photos, and theme toggle position all verified present).

## 1. New hero tagline reintroduces the speed/AI-hype risk it was meant to fix
- **Claim:** "To Compete In AI Race, Requires Racing at AI Speed"
- **Why it matters:** This replaced a generic-but-neutral line with one that foregrounds pure speed — exactly the lever this buyer has learned to distrust ("a faster tool or flashier AI feature misses the point"). It's also generic: "move fast to compete" is table-stakes AI marketing, not a structural diagnosis, so it doesn't yet differentiate this firm from any AI hype vendor.
- **Proposed Fix:** Keep the competitive-pressure framing (it does speak to real market pressure this buyer feels) but anchor it to structure, not raw speed: *"Everyone's racing toward AI. Few have the operating model to run at that speed safely. We build yours."*

## 2. Still zero evidence anywhere — and the company's own name promises it
- **Gap:** No case study, client, number, or outcome anywhere across home, Knowledge, Hands-On Presence, or Team.
- **Why it matters:** The firm is literally named "Resilient ROI," yet nothing on the site shows an ROI figure or outcome. This gap is now more conspicuous than in the last review, precisely because the brand name promises exactly what's missing.
- **Proposed Fix:** One verifiable data point anywhere — unchanged suggestion, still open.

## 3. DBJ Method's ownership relative to Resilient ROI Ltd is unstated where buyers look
- **Gap:** The Team page presents Dusan as author of "a hand-built AI operating model" and the nav links out to method.dbj.org as what reads like a separate site, but never states the relationship — that DBJ Method is Dusan's personal IP, licensed to Resilient ROI Ltd as its foundational operating model (that relationship only appears on `/dbj_method_ip/`, a legal page most buyers won't reach from Team).
- **Why it matters:** A buyer doing diligence on "who's behind this" is left wondering whether Resilient ROI owns its core method, is reselling someone else's framework, or depends entirely on one person's personal IP — a continuity risk for a buyer committing to a multi-year operating model.
- **Proposed Fix:** One sentence on the Team page: "Dusan is the author of the DBJ Method; Resilient ROI operates under it as its foundational IP," linking to `/dbj_method_ip/`.

## 4. Mahmudul Hoque's bio has content now, but no verifiable credential
- **Gap:** His bio (added since the last review) has no named employer, certification, or link a buyer could check — unlike Dusan's bio, which now links to an actual TOGAF certificate image.
- **Why it matters:** The contrast is sharper now, not softer: one founder's claims are checkable, the other's aren't.
- **Proposed Fix:** Add one verifiable detail if one exists — a named past employer, a LinkedIn profile, a certification.

**Resolved since the last review:** the Knowledge homepage card and page headings now lead with plain language (acronyms follow) — the jargon finding is closed. Entity naming is now consistent "Resilient ROI Ltd" everywhere — that nit is closed too.

Resilient ROI Limited (company no. 17415109, registered office 36 Ludlow Avenue, Luton, England, LU1 3RW) is the data controller for this site.

## What we collect

This site is static: no cookies, no analytics, no tracking scripts, no forms.

The only personal data we receive is what you send us yourself, by emailing [info@resilientroi.com](mailto:info@resilientroi.com) — your email address and whatever you include in the message.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As the hosting provider, GitHub processes standard web server request logs (such as IP address, browser type, and request time) to serve pages; we do not access or use these logs ourselves.

## What we do with it

We use the email you send us only to reply to you. We do not share it with third parties, and we do not use it for marketing.

## Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. Contact [info@resilientroi.com](mailto:info@resilientroi.com).

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/).

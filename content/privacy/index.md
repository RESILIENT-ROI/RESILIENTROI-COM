## Who we are

Resilient ROI Limited, registered in England and Wales, company number 17415109. Registered office: 36 Ludlow Avenue, Luton, LU1 3RW. For anything about your personal data, write to [info@resilientroi.com](mailto:info@resilientroi.com).

## What this website collects

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking on this website, and we set no cookies.

- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data about your visit, such as your IP address, to run and secure the service.
- **Fonts.** Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when a page loads.
- **Light or dark display.** If you switch between light and dark display, your browser stores that choice locally. It is not sent to us.

Our lawful basis for the hosting and font processing is our legitimate interest in delivering a working, secure website. GitHub and Google are based in the United States, so this data may be processed there under those providers' own transfer safeguards.

## When you email us

If you write to us, we use your name, email address and what you send us to reply and to deal with your enquiry. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering the people who contact us. Your messages are held by our email provider. We keep them only as long as we need them for that purpose, and we do not sell or share them for marketing.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it.

You also have the right to object to our use of your data, and to ask us to restrict how we use it.

To use any of these rights, write to [info@resilientroi.com](mailto:info@resilientroi.com).

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk/).

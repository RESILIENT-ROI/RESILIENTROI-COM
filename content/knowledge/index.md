Five courses, lecture and presentation based. No live instance, no tooling — just the model, so your people understand it before they run it.

Large organisations, where many people and roles are involved, typically start here. Smaller teams can often skip straight to [Hands-on](/hands-on/) and pick up what they need as they go.

## 1. DBJ Method

An overview of the whole method: how Taxonomy, CMM, ADM and BPT fit together, and the order in which an organisation adopts them. The map before the detail.

## 2. Taxonomy

The shared vocabulary every role — business, product and technology — uses to talk about the same thing. A four-by-four hierarchy of Categories and Capabilities, fixed and not open to local reinterpretation, so the business can navigate its own information space without inventing new terms for it as it goes.

## 3. CMM — Capability Maturity Model

How organisational maturity is measured, on a scale of L0 to L5. Covers the five elements maturity is scored against, the entry gate (a personal exam before anyone works inside BPT), and why the method treats the maturity level as the organisation's own KPI — not what it delivered, but what it is now able to deliver.

## 4. ADM — The Wheel

The decision cycle each domain runs: five steps, cut down from TOGAF's own ADM, each producing one signed-off document. Covers what each step decides, what it must leave behind in writing, and how a cycle closes.

## 5. BPT — Business, Product and Technology

The operating loop itself: three decoupled domains running in parallel, each owning one ADM wheel, communicating asynchronously in two cycles — Business with Product, Product with Technology. Covers how the loop stays honest without either side waiting on the other.

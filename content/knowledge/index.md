Five courses, lecture and presentation based. No live instance, no tooling — just the model, so your people understand it before they run it.

Large organisations, where many people and roles are involved, typically start here. Smaller teams can often skip straight to [Hands-On Presence](/hands-on/) and pick up what they need as they go.

## 1. [DBJ Method](https://method.dbj.org/)

An overview of the whole method: how Taxonomy, the Capability Maturity Model, the Architecture Development Method and Business, Product and Technology fit together, and the order in which an organisation adopts them. The fly over map before the detail.

## 2. [Taxonomy](https://method.dbj.org/taxonomy/index.html)

The shared vocabulary every role — in every domain: business, product and technology — uses to understand they talk about the same thing. A four-by-four hierarchy of Categories and Capabilities, fixed and not open to local reinterpretation, so the business can navigate its own information space without inventing new terms for it as it goes.

## 3. [CMM — Capability Maturity Model](https://method.dbj.org/cmm/index.html)

How organisational maturity is measured, on a common scale. Covers the five elements maturity is scored against, the entry gate (a personal exam before anyone works inside BPT), and why the method treats the maturity level as the organisation's own KPI — not what it delivered, but what it is now able to deliver.

## 4. [ADM — The Wheel](https://method.dbj.org/adm/index.html)

The decision cycle each domain runs: five steps, cut down from [TOGAF](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf)'s own ADM, each producing one signed-off document. Covers what each step decides, what it must leave behind in writing, and how a cycle closes.

## 5. [BPT — Business, Product and Technology](https://method.dbj.org/bpt/index.html)

The operating loop itself: three decoupled domains running in parallel, each owning one ADM wheel, communicating asynchronously in two cycles — Business with Product, Product with Technology. Covers how the loop stays honest without either side waiting on the other.

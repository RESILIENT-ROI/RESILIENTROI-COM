We work inside your own teams, on your premises, to set up and run the thing itself — not just teach it. Day-to-day administration is handled with our own tool, BPTAPP.

Smaller teams can often start here directly: BPTAPP handles instance administration without requiring your whole organisation to go through [Knowledge](/knowledge/) training first. Larger organisations, with more people and roles to align first, usually start with Knowledge and come here once their people are ready.

## 1. BPT instance: setup, running, monitoring

One BPT instance is a production line for one digital product: three decoupled domains — Business, Product, Technology — each running its own ADM wheel of five deliverables, communicating asynchronously in two cycles so neither side waits on the other. We set the instance up from our own template, bring your people's maturity to the level the method requires to start, and keep it running and monitored with BPTAPP.

## 2. Factory: setup, running, monitoring

Once an organisation runs more than one BPT instance, it needs a Factory: the layer shared across every instance — workforce CMM training records, the organisation's own description — so each product team draws on one consistent base instead of duplicating it. We set the Factory up, and keep it running and monitored alongside the instances it serves.

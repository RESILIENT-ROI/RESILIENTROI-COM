Raising your crew's maturity, from boarding to following the operating model. The result is a steady stream of AI-enabled products or services, all resilient, safe and compliant.

## The operating model

Every project we run follows the [DBJ Method](https://method.dbj.org/). It organises the work into three domains:

- **Business** approves the direction of the work and specifies what the product must do.
- **Products** turns that into requirements, a design and a backlog, and takes the result through user acceptance testing.
- **Technology** builds what Products defines.

The three domains work in a loop, not a linear hand-off chain: Business requires, Technology develops, the product is evaluated, refined and redeployed until it meets the agreed standard. Every requirement is tracked and traced, and every deliverable references the requirements it satisfies.

## What adoption commits an organisation to

Adoption commits an organisation to four things:

- **Architecture is everyone's contribution.** Every domain is responsible for understanding how its decisions affect the whole system.
- **Maturity over complexity.** Climb the maturity ladder progressively; each level must be stable before the next begins.
- **Cycle, don't waterfall.** The Business, Products, Technology loop replaces linear delivery.
- **Write the why down.** A Business Decision Record keeps the reasoning behind each decision, so it outlives the people who made it.

The method names the domains, the roles, the decisions they own and the loop they run. It does not prescribe a tool: you keep what you already run, or choose your own.

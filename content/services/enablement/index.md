Raising your crews' maturity, from boarding to following the operating model. The result is a steady stream of AI-enabled products or services, all resilient, safe and compliant.

## Organisation first, then AI

In the [DBJ Method](https://method.dbj.org/), organisation maturity comes before AI maturity. The order is deliberate:

1. **Organisation maturity.** Roles, standard operating procedures and decision rights are made clear, so people know their work.
2. **Technical debt removal.** Data schemas are cleaned up, brittle silos are removed, and legacy code is refactored. The legacy knowledge is kept; the old technology is retired.
3. **AI maturity.** AI is applied only to workflows that are already stable and understood.

AI applied before those foundations are in place multiplies technical debt rather than reducing it.

## How an engagement starts

Every engagement begins with training for a cross-functional team, covering all roles. The team learns the [Common Taxonomy](https://method.dbj.org/taxonomy/index.html), so business, product and technology people use one vocabulary, and works through the [DBJ Capability Maturity Model](https://method.dbj.org/cmm/index.html). Each person passes the maturity exam to enter the BPT loop. Contribution starts at level 3; level 5 is the target.

## Working inside the operating model

Once on board, the crew works in the **Business ↔ Products ↔ Technology** loop rather than in a linear delivery line:

- **Business** approves direction and declares the capabilities it needs.
- **Products** decomposes the work, builds it iteratively, evaluates it against the organisation's context and deploys it for acceptance testing.
- **Technology** implements what Products defines, against the existing IT landscape.

Each segment runs its own [ADM wheel](https://method.dbj.org/adm/index.html). Every requirement is tracked and traced to the deliverables that satisfy it, and every decision is written down in a [Business Decision Record](https://method.dbj.org/adoption/bdr.html), so later exceptions can be traced back to their origin.

## What it does not prescribe

The method does not require a particular tool, workflow engine or board. You can build, buy or keep your existing project management system. The method specifies only what that system must capture: who decides, what is recorded, and where exceptions go.

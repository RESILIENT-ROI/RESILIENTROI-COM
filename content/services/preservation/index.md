Every mature organisation reaches the point where the business value it holds is worth more than the software it has built or uses. Your old systems hold years of business knowledge. We enable your teams to take it out and keep it safe before anything is replaced, so modernisation does not throw it away with the old code.

## Technical debt as an iceberg

The legacy technology is the visible tip. Most of the value sits below it: the rules, exceptions and judgement the business has built up over the years. Technical debt is business value imprisoned in the iceberg of legacy technology. The aim is to break the iceberg apart so that value can be reused.

Refactoring the code alone does not release that knowledge. It tends to become a slow, wide cycle that keeps returning to where it started.

## Separate the knowledge from the technology

Preservation follows the [DBJ Method](https://method.dbj.org/) and its [Business ↔ Products ↔ Technology](https://method.dbj.org/bpt/) domains. Each role works where it belongs: business roles in the Business domain, business analysts in the Products domain, engineers and DevOps in the Technology domain.

1. **Initiative.** The business starts an initiative to remove legacy systems.
2. **Declare the problems.** Business and Products identify the legacy processes and the technical debt.
3. **Store the knowledge.** Products creates a place where the knowledge will be kept.
4. **Choose the products.** The business decides on the new products and selects the first one.
5. **Declare the project.** Business and Products declare a new project.
6. **Decouple the knowledge.** Business analysts separate the knowledge from legacy processes; Products and Technology separate it from legacy components. This repeats until no more legacy decoupling is needed.
7. **Decide the architecture.** Business and analysts decide the conceptual and logical architecture; analysts and engineers the physical architecture; Technology the application architecture.
8. **Deliver.** Engineers start the project cycles.

## Which debt to service

Not every debt has to be cleared first. Business leaders need to know which debts they can live with, and for how long, which one would undermine the AI investment, and what it will cost to service the rest.

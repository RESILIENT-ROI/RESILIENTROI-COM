For architecture practices developing a method based on ours. Only the parts of the DBJ Method they need, one chapter at a time. We encapsulated TOGAF so that any role distant from architecture sees benefits, not confusion.

## What the method is built from

The DBJ Method's maturity model, the DBJ CMM, is the TOGAF CMM tailored for businesses: all roles, not only architects. It uses the same scale as the TOGAF Architecture Capability Maturity Model and maps onto it one to one. Where TOGAF's model assesses the architecture practice, the DBJ CMM assesses the whole organisation.

## One chapter at a time

The [DBJ Guide](https://guide.dbj.org/) mirrors the method's structure: a foreword and four chapters.

1. **Taxonomy:** classify to find, name to understand.
2. **CMM:** continuously raise your team's maturity.
3. **Projects steering in practice.**
4. **BPT:** decoupled to deliver faster.

The AI primer sits alongside them. It is not a chapter of its own, because AI is overarching.

Each use case comes in two stages, standard and advanced. Every term is defined once, on the [DBJ Method](https://method.dbj.org/), and the Guide shows how to use it.

Most customers suffer the technical debt.

Every organisation not operating on a simple and usable methodology struggled to deliver ROI even before AI arrived. AI arrival has amplified the dysfunction.

## Knowledge Preservation

Technical Debt is Business Value imprisoned in the Iceberg of Legacy Technology.

Break that Iceberg and free your business value.

Refactoring is a very wide and slow circle of pain, always leading back to the beginning.

## Which debt kills the AI investment

Customers quickly spot where Resilient ROI earn their money. Not by demanding poor engineers to clear every debt first, but by telling business leaders:

- which debts they can live with,
- and for how long,
- which one kills the AI investment,
- and what it will actually cost to service the rest of the company landscape.

## No need to dive into the code

The customer IT landscape is analysed across all four abstraction levels: Conceptual, Logical, Physical and Implementation. At the upper levels of abstraction, anomalies, deficiencies and technical debt are observed, and that stops further descent down the abstractions.

No need to dive into the code if design is wrong.

## ROI follows

The BPT method addresses the legacy operational problems first. ROI follows naturally, and AI-induced ROI follows from that, not the other way around.
